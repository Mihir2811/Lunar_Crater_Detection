MoonCrunch

MoonCrunch is a project developed to facilitate automated identification and analysis of lunar impact craters from orbital imagery. The system is intended for research, validation, and analytical workflows in planetary science and remote sensing domains.

How It Works

Three simple steps to crater detection

01

Image Submission

Upload lunar surface imagery acquired from orbital missions or telescopic observations.

02

Automated Processing

The system applies a trained detection model to identify and classify crater formations.

03

Result Generation

Annotated imagery and structured crater metadata are generated for download and further analysis.
